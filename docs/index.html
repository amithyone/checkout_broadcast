Why Checkout Broadcast

The fastest way to start a transfer at the counter

Transfers are how Nigeria pays in shops. Checkout Broadcast removes the slow, error-prone part: getting the right recipient and amount into the customer's app.

No typing, no scanning

No 10-digit account numbers, no bank picker, no camera. The customer opens their app, taps the shop, and confirms an amount the till already set.

Type account number
Pick the bank
Type the amount
Tap shop, confirm with PIN

Signed by the till

Every request carries an Ed25519 signature over the terminal, amount and session. Change the amount and verification fails.

Verified by the bank

The bank checks the terminal registry, replay and timestamps before the app shows the verified merchant name.

Works with the rails you already run

No new settlement to certify. Checkout Broadcast only changes how a transfer starts; the payment itself is a normal NIP transfer with the session ID as the idempotency key. It complements NQR rather than replacing it.

How it works

From checkout to paid in three steps

1

Till broadcasts

A Windows or Linux till totals the sale, signs terminal ID, amount and a one-time session with Ed25519, and broadcasts compact {p, alg, sig} over Bluetooth LE.

2

Bank verifies

The customer's app lists only the tills in range of the phone, and the customer taps theirs. The app sends that packet to its bank's /verify-broadcast. The bank checks signature, registry and replay, and returns the merchant.

3

Customer confirms

The transfer opens pre-filled with a locked amount and the verified shop name. Nothing is picked or paid automatically: the customer confirms with their PIN and the bank sends a normal transfer.
