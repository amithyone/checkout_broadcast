Pay at the counter without typing an account number.

Checkout Broadcast is an open protocol and SDK. The shop's till broadcasts a signed payment request over Bluetooth LE, the customer's banking app verifies it with the bank, and the transfer screen is pre-filled with the right merchant and amount. The money still moves over the rails banks already use.

How it works

STEP 1 · TILL

Checkout and broadcast

The POS totals the sale, signs terminal ID, amount and a one-time session with its Ed25519 key, and broadcasts it over BLE.

STEP 2 · BANK

Verify

The customer's app sends the packet to its bank's /verify-broadcast. The bank checks the signature, terminal registry and replay, and returns the merchant name and account.

STEP 3 · CUSTOMER

Confirm and pay

The transfer screen opens pre-filled with a locked amount. The customer confirms with their PIN and the bank sends a normal transfer.

Security in one paragraph

The till signs the expanded payload with Ed25519; the bank stores only the public key. Changing the amount breaks the signature. A fake till fails because its terminal isn't registered. Replayed packets fail because each checkout session UUID is burned after use and stale timestamps are rejected. Packets are signed, not encrypted, so they only carry a masked account suffix. Full threat model in SECURITY.md.
